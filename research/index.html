My current research is focused on capturing intratumor heterogeneity of liver metastases with radiology. Briefly I’m trying to answer the following questions:

  • Can we capture intratumor heterogeneity?
  • If yes, is this intratumor heterogeneity clinically relevant?

Note that intratumor heterogeneity doesn’t have the same meaning at different scales. This review is a very good starting point if you’re interested in the topic.
