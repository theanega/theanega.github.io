I am a biomedical engineer. I recently defended my PhD thesis at the [Radiomics Group](https://radiomicsgroup.github.io/) at [Vall d’Hebron Institute of Oncology (VHIO)](https://vhio.net/), where I studied CT-based habitat imaging to quantify tumor heterogeneity in colorectal liver metastases.

I am currently on a career break exploring the [problem](/2026/05/01/the-data-we-already-have.html) of health data infrastructure: specifically, why clinical data that already exists doesn’t reach research, and whether giving patients agency over their own data could change that. If you're interested in discussing this idea or have any insights on how to move it forward, I would love to talk to you. My email is [theanega@gmail.com](mailto:theanega@gmail.com).
