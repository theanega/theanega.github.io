## Other ideas I'd like to see people build
- Better apps for learning how to play the ukulele (especially strumming patterns)
- Something that helps you identify what you like and something that encourages other people describe what you're good at
- An improvement of Google Reviews for tourism. I don't trust Google reviews anymore. I'd love something that filters recommendations through friends instead of strangers. I want to see which places my friend P goes to because her taste is different from mine, and which places my friend X goes to because we're alike.
