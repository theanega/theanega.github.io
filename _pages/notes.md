This space serves as my digital garden — a collection of evolving notes on different subjects. By sharing this garden publicly I hope to be more intentional about my writing: improve clarity, be consistent with tags, and overall document how my thinking evolves over time. 

Writing is a great way to think, to make room for new thoughts and to help others who might benefit from it. Some people whose writings have helped me in different ways and who have insipired me are: Donella Meadows, Nadia Asparouhova (former Eghbal), Devon Zuegel, Neel Nanda, Michael Nielsen, Alexey Guzey, José Luis Rincón, Maggie Appleton, Terence Tao, Andy Matuschak, la chica que no tiene muchos follower, Gavin Leech, Lucas Amaro, Simon Sarris and Paul Graham.
